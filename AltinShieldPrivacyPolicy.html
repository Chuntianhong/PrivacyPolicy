01 Introduction #

ID Shield (“we”, “our”, “us”) provides Altin Shield (the “App”), a workforce management tool designed for the Australian security industry.

We are committed to respecting your privacy and being transparent about how information is handled when you use our App.

02 Nature of the App (Local Data Storage) #

Altin Shield is designed as a local data storage tool.

All information entered into the App is stored locally on your device.

We do not collect, transmit, store, or have access to this information on our servers.

03 Information Stored in the App #

Users may choose to store personal and sensitive information within the App, including but not limited to:

  • Names and contact details
  • Security licences and compliance certificates
  • Identification documents (e.g. driver licences, passports)
  • Training and employment records

This information is entered and managed entirely by the user or their organisation.

04 Our Role #

We act solely as a software provider. We:

  • Do not access your data
  • Do not process your data
  • Do not share your data

All data remains under the control of the user.

05 Data Security #

As data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your own device protections. We recommend that users:

  • Enable device passwords or biometric locks
  • Restrict device access to authorised personnel
  • Keep devices secure at all times

We are not responsible for unauthorised access resulting from compromised devices.

06 Data Loss #

Because data is not stored on our servers:

  • We do not provide backup or recovery services
  • If your device is lost, damaged, or reset, your data may be permanently lost

Users are responsible for maintaining their own backups where necessary.

07 Device Permissions #

The App may request access to certain device features, such as:

  • Storage — to save and retrieve documents
  • Camera — to capture and upload identification or certification documents

These permissions are used solely to enable App functionality. We do not access or collect this data.

08 Third-Party Services #

The App does not use third-party analytics, tracking tools, or external services that collect personal information.

09 Your Responsibilities #

If you are using the App to manage information about employees or contractors, you are responsible for complying with applicable privacy laws, including the Privacy Act 1988 and any relevant industry obligations.

10 Changes to This Policy #

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Effective Date” above.
