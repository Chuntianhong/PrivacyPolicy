Getting started

  1. Install the app. Search for “Altin Shield” on the App Store and tap Get.
  2. Open and accept permissions. When prompted, allow Camera and Photos so you can capture and attach licences and certificates.
  3. Add your first record. Create a profile and attach the documents you need to keep on hand.
  4. Keep your device secure. Enable a passcode or biometric lock so your records stay protected.

Frequently asked questions

What is Altin Shield?

Altin Shield is a workforce management app designed for the Australian security industry. It helps you store and manage worker records, licences, certificates, and ID documents.

Which devices and iOS versions are supported?

Altin Shield runs on iPhone and iPad. We recommend keeping iOS up to date and using the latest App Store version of Altin Shield to receive bug fixes and improvements.

Where is my data stored?

Most information is stored locally on your device. When you choose to upload or sync files through the App, those files may be stored using secure cloud infrastructure operated by trusted third-party providers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Why does the app need Camera or Photo access?

Camera access lets you capture identification or certification documents directly. Photo access lets you attach existing images. Both permissions are optional and only used when you add a document.

How do I back up my records?

If you only keep records on the device, they may be lost if the device is lost or reset. Use the App’s upload or export options where available, or maintain your own copies, especially before changing devices.

How do I restore data on a new device?

Install Altin Shield on the new device and sign in or import data using whichever option is available in the App. If you only kept records locally on the old device, those records may not be recoverable.

How do I delete my data or my account?

Inside the App you can remove individual records. To request deletion of any associated account data or stored uploads, contact us at tfprooffice@gmail.com from the email associated with your account.

The app crashes or behaves unexpectedly — what should I do?

Try the following:

  • Close and re-open the App.
  • Restart your device.
  • Update to the latest version on the App Store.
  • If the issue continues, email us with your device model, iOS version, App version, and a short description of what you were doing.
How do I report a security issue?

Please email tfprooffice@gmail.com with the subject “Security report” and as much detail as you can share. We take security reports seriously and will respond as quickly as possible.

Is Altin Shield free?

The App is available on the App Store. Pricing or in-app purchases (if any) are shown on the App Store listing.

Troubleshooting tips

  • Camera not working? Open Settings → Altin Shield and confirm Camera access is enabled.
  • Photos not appearing? Check Settings → Altin Shield → Photos and select “All Photos” or “Selected Photos” as needed.
  • Upload failing? Confirm you have a stable internet connection. Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
  • App won’t open? Restart the device. If the issue continues, reinstall from the App Store (note: data not exported or uploaded may be lost).
